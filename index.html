3D Slicer Configuration Reference (Orca-Flashforge / OrcaSlicer)

Recommended profiles for 0.4 mm nozzle on high-speed CoreXY printers (e.g., Flashforge AD5X)

1. Lithophane Lamp Shades (Image Embossed)

Lithophanes rely on 100% solid plastic density so light transmission is controlled strictly by wall thickness—any hollow infill grid will cast unwanted shadows.

Wall Generator: Arachne (Essential for variable micro-relief)
Layer Height: 0.12 mm – 0.16 mm (0.20 mm first layer)
Wall Loops (Perimeters): 7 to 9 loops (or enough to exceed max thickness)
Sparse Infill Density: 100% (Rectilinear)
Outer Wall Speed: 40 – 60 mm/s (prevents ringing on fine details)
Seam Position: Aligned or Back (place at the back of the image)
Filament: Warm White / Natural PLA or Matte Ivory PLA
Supports: None required (spokes print via bridging; add a 5 mm outer brim if tall)

2. Geometric, Ribbed & Twisted Shades (Non-Lithophane)

App Wall Thickness: 1.2 mm – 1.6 mm
Layer Height: 0.16 mm – 0.20 mm
Wall Loops: 3 to 4 loops (0% Infill to avoid internal grid lines)
Filament: Translucent PETG, Silk PLA, Wood PLA, or Matte PLA

3. Spiral Vase Mode Workflow

Set Attachment Style to Solid Base. In Orca-Flashforge, enable Spiral Vase, set Bottom Shell Layers to 4–6, and set Outer Wall Line Width to 0.6 mm – 0.8 mm (even with a 0.4 mm nozzle) for a sturdy single-wall shade. To open a socket hole in the base, right-click the model in your slicer → Add Negative Part → Cylinder sized to your socket diameter.

Thermal Safety Note: Standard PLA softens around 60°C (140°F). Always use low-wattage LED bulbs (under 9W–12W LED equivalent). If using higher-output bulbs or enclosed fixtures, print the shade or fitter in PETG or ASA.
